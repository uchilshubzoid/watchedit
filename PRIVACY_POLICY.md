# Privacy Policy — WatchedIt

**Last updated: June 2026**

---

## Overview

WatchedIt is a personal watch tracking app that lets you log, rate, and remember everything you watch. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your data.

**Short version: WatchedIt has no servers of its own. Your data is stored on your device. If you choose to enable Google Drive sync, your data is also backed up to your own Google Drive — it goes directly there, not to us.**

---

## Data We Collect

WatchedIt itself does not collect any personal data and does not operate any servers.

All information you enter — titles you've logged, ratings, reactions, watch history, and preferences — is stored on your device. This data is never transmitted to WatchedIt or any WatchedIt-operated service.

If you choose to sign in with Google and enable Drive sync (this is optional), your WatchLog data is also written to a private folder in your own Google Drive. See the Google Drive Sync section below for details.

---

## Data Stored On Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device:

- Your watcher name (display name set during onboarding)
- Titles you have logged, including status, ratings, reactions, and watch dates
- App preferences such as title language preference and custom categories
- Onboarding completion status
- If Drive sync is enabled: your Google account email address, an OAuth access token, and the timestamp of your last sync

This data is stored using Android's local storage and is only accessible within the app.

---

## Google Drive Sync (Optional)

WatchedIt offers an optional Google Drive sync feature. If you choose to use it:

- You sign in with your Google account. WatchedIt requests access to your Google account email and profile (for display purposes) and to a private app-specific folder in your Google Drive (`drive.appdata` scope).
- Your WatchLog data is written to that private folder in your own Google Drive. This folder is not visible in the regular Google Drive file browser — it is only accessible to WatchedIt.
- WatchedIt does not have access to any other files in your Google Drive.
- Your data goes directly from your device to your Google Drive. It does not pass through WatchedIt's servers (WatchedIt has no servers).
- Google processes your sign-in and stores the backed-up data as part of your Google account. Google's privacy policy applies to that data: policies.google.com/privacy

**To disconnect:** Open the Watcher screen → tap Unlink under Google Drive. This removes the connection from the app. To also delete the backed-up data from Google Drive, go to Google Drive settings → Manage apps → WatchedIt → Delete hidden app data.

Google Drive sync is entirely optional. The app is fully functional without it.

## Third Party Services

WatchedIt uses the following third party services:

- **The Movie Database (TMDB)** — for movie and TV show search and metadata. Search queries are sent directly from your device to TMDB. See TMDB's privacy policy at themoviedb.org/privacy-policy
- **MyAnimeList (MAL)** — for anime search and metadata. Search queries are sent directly from your device to MAL. See MAL's privacy policy at myanimelist.net/about/privacy_policy
- **OMDb API** — for additional movie metadata. Search queries are sent directly from your device to OMDb. See OMDb's privacy policy at omdbapi.com
- **Google Sign-In / Google Drive** — used only if you choose to enable Drive sync. See Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy

These services may log API requests as part of their standard operations. WatchedIt does not store or have access to those logs.

## Data Deletion

**Device data:** Uninstalling WatchedIt removes all locally stored data from your device.

**Google Drive data (if Drive sync was enabled):** Uninstalling the app does not automatically delete the backup stored in your Google Drive. To delete it, go to Google Drive settings → Manage apps → WatchedIt → Delete hidden app data. You can also disconnect Drive sync from within the app (Watcher screen → Unlink) before uninstalling.
