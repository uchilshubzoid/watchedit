# Privacy Policy — WatchedIt

**Last updated: May 2026**

---

## Overview

WatchedIt is a personal watch tracking app that lets you log, rate, and remember everything you watch. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your data.

**Short version: WatchedIt stores all your data locally on your device. We don't collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers.**

---

## Data We Collect

WatchedIt does not collect any personal data.

All information you enter into the app — including titles you've logged, ratings, reactions, watch history, and preferences — is stored exclusively on your device using local storage. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party.

---

## Data Stored On Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device only:

- Your watcher name (display name you set during onboarding)
- Titles you have logged, including status, ratings, reactions, and watch dates
- App preferences such as title language preference and custom categories
- Onboarding completion status

This data is stored using Android's local storage and is only accessible within the app.

---

## Third Party Services

WatchedIt uses the following third party APIs to search for titles when you use the Log It feature. These requests are made directly from your device to the respective services:

- **The Movie Database (TMDB)** — for movie and TV show search and metadata. See TMDB's privacy policy at themoviedb.org/privacy-policy
- **MyAnimeList (MAL)** — for anime search and metadata. See MAL's privacy policy at myanimelist.net/about/privacy_policy
- **OMDb API** — for additional movie metadata. See OMDb's privacy policy at omdbapi.com

These services may log your search queries as part of their standard API usage. WatchedIt does not store or have access to these logs.

---

## Advertising

WatchedIt contains no advertisements.

---

## Analytics

WatchedIt does not use any analytics tools or tracking services.

---

## Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app. Uninstalling WatchedIt permanently removes all locally stored data.

---

## Children's Privacy

WatchedIt does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children. The app contains no data collection mechanisms of any kind.
